The app

A clerk for your cause list.

In Punjab's district courts, a hearing date can move without warning. Munshi does what a diligent munshi would: it watches the court's case-search site every day, notes the next date for each of your cases, and keeps a matching event on your Google Calendar.

You add a case once. From then on Munshi handles the checking, the moving, and the rebuilding, quietly in the background, so the date is always where you expect it.

What it does

Built so a case is never quietly unprotected.

Munshi's one job is that you never miss a hearing, so the sync has no silent holes. A case is either covered by a real event or flagged for your attention.

01

Tracks a real event

Each case is tied to an actual Google Calendar event that Munshi creates and remembers, not a guess from a title.

02

Follows adjournments

When a hearing moves to a new date, Munshi moves the same event. No stale copy left behind, no duplicate.

03

Self-heals

Delete the event by mistake and the next run rebuilds it. A deleted event can't cost you a hearing.

04

Your edits win

Rename the event or change its reminder in Google Calendar and those edits carry forward to future hearings.

05

Keeps history

Once a hearing date passes, that event stays put and a fresh one is made for the next date. Munshi never deletes anything.

06

Runs quietly

Once a day in the background, and it can start at login. No window to keep open, nothing to remember.

How it's built

Dependable, not clever.

Munshi is an Electron desktop app. No Python, no browser driver, nothing extra to install. A hidden window reads the court site, captcha and all, in the same process, and the sync engine that decides to create, move, or rebuild an event is pure, deterministic TypeScript covered by unit tests.

Google Calendar is the source of truth. Sign-in uses the OAuth 2.0 authorization-code flow with PKCE, so your password is only ever typed on Google's own page.

Questions

Good to know

Is Munshi free?+
Yes. Munshi is completely free to download and use.
Which courts does it cover?+
Munshi reads hearing dates from the District Judiciary Punjab case-search website (dsjlahore.punjab.gov.pk). Coverage there is currently focused on Lahore and grows as the site adds districts.
Is my Google data safe?+
Yes. Munshi runs entirely on your own computer, so there is no Munshi server. Your Google tokens and case data never leave your device, and it only touches the events it creates or that you link to a case. Full detail is in the privacy policy.
Do I need to keep the app open?+
No. Munshi checks your cases once a day in the background and can start quietly at login. Because it is a desktop app rather than a server, the daily run happens whenever your PC is on.
What are the system requirements?+
A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC and a Google account. The installer is a standard Windows setup with no extra runtimes to install.
Will it change events I made myself?+
No. Munshi only manages the event it created for each tracked case, or one you explicitly link. Any edits you make to it are respected and carried forward.
